Before the first file

Before any work starts, you walk us through how your team works its files. We ask our process questions, write a standard operating procedure (SOP) and send it to you for approval. We start only once you approve it.

Your team re-checks, then issues

We type everything so your team only has to re-check it, then get back to closings and client relationships. Your team re-checks our work before anything is issued and makes the final underwriting decisions. Jade does not run full title searches, issue commitments or policies, or give legal advice.

Confidential, and inside your software

Everything you send us is confidential and handled only by our own team. We work inside your software, so nothing is downloaded, copied out or kept on our side. Files are opened only in your system, or from documents you email us, and every file stays where you can revisit it.
