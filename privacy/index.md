This policy explains what information Idea Brothers LLC, doing business as Jade Title Services ("Jade", "we", "us"), collects through jadetitleservices.com, how we use it, and the choices you have. It also explains, in plain terms, how we treat the client files we work on.

Jade is an offshore title production team that provides back-office support to title companies, real estate attorneys and lenders in the United States. Our website is meant for those businesses. It is not directed at consumers or at children.

## 1. Information we collect through the website

**Information you give us.** When you fill in the contact or consultation form, email us or call us, we collect what you provide. This usually includes your name, work email address, phone number, company name, the title software you use, the service you are interested in, your approximate file volume and your message.

**Information collected automatically.** Like most websites, ours records basic technical information when you visit: IP address, browser and device type, pages viewed, the page that referred you, and the date and time of the visit. We use cookies or similar tools for analytics so we can see how the site is used.

We do not ask for sensitive personal information through the website. Please do not send client files, closing documents or any consumer's personal or financial information through the contact form.

## 2. How we use website information

We use the information above to:

- reply to your enquiry and prepare a quote;
- set up and manage a trial or an ongoing service relationship;
- send you service-related messages you have asked for;
- understand how the website is used and improve it;
- keep the website secure and prevent misuse;
- comply with the law.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it with third parties for their own advertising.

## 3. Client files and confidentiality

This section describes how we handle the title files we work on for our clients. The detailed terms are set out in each client's service agreement, which takes priority over this summary.

- **Confidential.** Everything a client sends us or gives us access to is treated as confidential.
- **Handled only by our team.** Client work is done by Jade's own team and no one else.
- **Kept inside the client's software.** We work inside the client's own title production system, using the access the client gives us. We do not download, copy out or keep client files on our side.
- **Limited access routes.** We open files only inside the client's software, or from documents the client chooses to email to us for a file.
- **The client's instructions.** We use client file information only to carry out the work the client has asked for, following the written procedure the client has approved.
- **The client stays in control.** Each client controls our user access in its own system and can change or remove it at any time.

When we work on client files, we do so on the client's behalf. The client decides what information is collected from its own customers and why. If you are a consumer and have a question about your information in a title or closing file, please contact the title company, attorney or lender handling your transaction.

## 4. Who we share website information with

We share website information only with:

- service providers that help us run the website and our business, such as hosting, email, form handling and analytics providers, who may use it only to provide those services to us;
- professional advisers, where needed;
- authorities, where the law requires it or where it is needed to protect our rights or the safety of others;
- a successor, if our business is reorganized or sold, on terms that keep this policy's protections.

## 5. International processing

Jade is an offshore team. Information you send us through the website is received and handled outside the United States. By contacting us, you understand that your information will be processed in the countries where we and our service providers operate.

## 6. How long we keep website information

We keep enquiry and contact information for as long as we need it to respond to you and to manage our relationship, and afterwards only as long as needed for our records or as the law requires. You can ask us to delete it sooner.

## 7. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect the information we hold. No website or method of transmission is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

## 8. Your choices

You can ask us to:

- tell you what personal information we hold about you;
- correct it;
- delete it;
- stop sending you messages.

To make a request, email info@jadetitleservices.com. We will respond within a reasonable time. Depending on where you live, you may have further rights under local law, and we will honor them.

You can set your browser to block cookies. The website will still work.

## 9. Links to other sites

Our website links to other sites, such as software vendors and news sources. We are not responsible for their content or privacy practices.

## 10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. When we do, we will change the date at the top. Significant changes will be noted on the website.
