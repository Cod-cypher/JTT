Our Services

Commitment typing is preparing the title commitment from search and examination results: Schedule A (proposed insured, policy amounts, vesting, and legal description), the requirements that must be met before a policy can issue, and the exceptions the policy won't cover. Learn more about commitment typing.

A title commitment is issued before closing and lists the requirements that must be satisfied and the exceptions to coverage. The final title policy is issued after closing and recording, once those requirements are met. Learn more about final policy preparation.

Search preparation gets a file ready for examination. That can include confirming the legal description and parcel ID, identifying every name to be searched, attaching prior policies, and organizing documents the way your examiners expect. Learn more about search preparation.

We work in industry-leading platforms, including Qualia, SoftPro, and RamQuest. If your office uses a different system, contact us. We adapt to existing systems and workflows.
