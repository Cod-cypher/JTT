How outsourced title support works

Jade Title Services works behind the scenes as an extension of your team. We do the production work in your title production software, following your procedures, templates, and underwriter requirements. Your team re-checks our work and keeps control of underwriting decisions, issuing, closings, and your customer relationships.

  1. Approve your SOP. You walk us through how you work your files, or send reference commitments and policies; we write a standard operating procedure and start only once you approve it.
  2. Send your files. Through your software or by email, the way your team already works.
  3. We do the production work. Inside your software, following the SOP you approved.
  4. Quality check. Every order goes through a quality assurance review before delivery.
  5. Back within 24 hours. Completed work comes back within our standard turnaround, ready for your team to re-check.
