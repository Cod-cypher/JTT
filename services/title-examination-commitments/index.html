What title examination and commitment typing cover

A title commitment is the title company's promise to issue a title insurance policy once certain conditions are met. It tells the buyer, the lender, and the closing team what has to happen before a policy can be issued. Examination is the review of the title search results to establish who owns the property and what affects it. Commitment typing enters those findings into the title commitment, in the format your underwriter requires.

What we prepare

Using the search package your team provides, we examine the title and type the commitment in your title production software, including:

  • Schedule A: the effective date, proposed insured, proposed policy amounts, current vested owner, and legal description.
  • Requirements (Schedule B, Part I): items that must be satisfied before the policy can issue, such as payoff and release of existing mortgages, satisfaction of judgments or liens, recording of the deed or mortgage, and any affidavits or documents needed.
  • Exceptions (Schedule B, Part II): matters the policy won't cover, such as recorded easements, covenants and restrictions, and taxes not yet due.

You supply the search package, re-check everything we do, make the final underwriting decisions and issue the commitment. Jade prepares searches but does not run full title searches, and is not a title insurer or underwriter.

Our quality check

Every commitment is reviewed before delivery. We check that names match the vesting deed and the order, that proposed policy amounts match the contract or loan, that the legal description carried over correctly, and that each item found in the examination appears as a requirement or an exception.
