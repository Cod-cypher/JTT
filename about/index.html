Why Jade exists

Jade was founded to give title companies an affordable back-office partner. Production work is what backs up first when volume rises, and it is the work that keeps skilled people away from closings and clients.

We type everything so your team only has to re-check it. You review our work before it goes out, then get back to closings and client relationships.

We are an offshore team and we say so plainly. It is how we keep rates low, cover evenings and weekends, and return files within 24 hours.

What we do, and what stays with you

We handle four services: title order entry, title search preparation, title examination and commitments, and final policies.

We examine and type; your team re-checks everything before it is issued. We prepare searches but do not run full title searches, and we do not issue commitments or policies. Your team makes the final underwriting decisions. Jade is not a title insurer, underwriter or law firm, and we do not give legal advice.

How we start with a new client

Before any work starts, you either walk us through how your team works its files or send us reference commitments and policies. From those we learn your wording and how you show each detail: deeds, mortgages, liens, chain of title, taxes and so on. We ask our process questions, write a standard operating procedure (SOP) and send it to you for approval. We start only once you approve it.

How we handle your files

  • Everything is confidential. Your files, your clients and your process are treated as confidential.
  • Only our team touches it. Your work is handled by our own team and no one else.
  • It stays in your software. We work inside your system. Nothing is downloaded, copied out or kept on our side.
  • Always there to revisit. Files are opened only in your software, or from documents you email us, so every file stays where you can go back to it.

Four values behind every file

  • Accuracy: a quality check on every order, and your re-check after ours.
  • Speed: a 24-hour standard turnaround, evenings and weekends included.
  • Partnership: we follow your SOP and work as part of your team.
  • Integrity: we say what we do and what we do not: no full searches, no issuing.
