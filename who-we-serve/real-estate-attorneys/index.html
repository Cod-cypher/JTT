Title work inside a law practice

In many areas, real estate attorneys conduct closings and also act as agents for title insurance underwriters. That means a law office carries both the legal work and the title production: opening files, preparing commitments, and issuing final policies after closing. The production side often lands on paralegals who are also coordinating closings, preparing documents, and answering client calls.

Where we fit

Jade Title Services takes on the repeatable production tasks so your staff can focus on the work that needs them:

  • Order entry: opening and setting up new title files from the contract or lender order.
  • Title search preparation: organizing search materials so the file is ready for the search. We prepare searches; we do not run them.
  • Title examination and commitments: examining your search package and typing the commitment in your underwriter's format.
  • Final policies: preparing owner's and lender's policies after closing and recording.

You stay in control

We're a title support service, not a law firm, and we don't give legal advice. The title opinion, underwriting decisions, final review and issuing remain yours. We make sure the documents reflect them accurately and consistently, and every item is reviewed before it comes back to your office.

Built around a busy practice

Closings don't keep office hours. We offer flexible hours, including evenings and weekends, and our standard turnaround is 24 hours. We work in common title production platforms such as Qualia, SoftPro, and RamQuest, and we adapt to the way your office already works.
