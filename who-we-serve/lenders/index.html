Why title production matters to lenders

For a lender, title work shows up at two key points. The first is the commitment, which has to be accurate and on time for underwriting and closing. The second is the final lender's policy, which the loan file needs after closing. A delay at either point leaves conditions open, creates extra follow-up for your post-closing team, and frustrates borrowers.

Most of that work happens inside the title company or attorney's office handling the transaction. When those offices are short-staffed, lenders feel it.
