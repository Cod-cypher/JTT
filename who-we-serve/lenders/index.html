Why title production matters to lenders

For a lender, title work shows up at two key points. The first is the commitment, which has to be accurate and on time for underwriting and closing. The second is the final lender's policy, which the loan file needs after closing. A delay at either point leaves conditions open, creates extra follow-up for your post-closing team, and frustrates borrowers.

Most of that work happens inside the title company or attorney's office handling the transaction. When those offices are short-staffed, lenders feel it.

How we support the title side of your loans

  • Title examination and commitments: accurate commitments where the proposed insured, loan amount, and requirements match the loan.
  • Final policies: lender's policies prepared after closing and recording, then reviewed against the commitment and closing documents.
  • Order entry and search preparation: files set up correctly from the day the order arrives.

Working with lenders and their title partners

Jade Title Services provides back-office support to the title companies and attorney offices that handle title work. If your organization has an affiliated title agency or an in-house title operation, we can work with that team directly. If you rely on outside title partners, you're welcome to point them to us.

What you can expect

A standard turnaround of 24 hours, a quality review on every order, and a team that works in common title production software, including Qualia, SoftPro, and RamQuest.
