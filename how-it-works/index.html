How working with Jade works

First we learn your process and you approve it in writing. Then files go out and come back within 24 hours, ready for you to re-check.

Get a free consultation
Step one: onboarding

We learn how you work before we touch a file

Every title company does things its own way. So we start by writing yours down, and no work begins until you have approved it.

  1. 1

    You show us how you work

    Walk us through a file in your own software, or send us reference commitments and policies. From those we learn your wording and how you show each detail: deeds, mortgages, liens, chain of title, taxes and so on.

  2. 2

    We ask, then draft your SOP

    We raise every question about your process up front, then write it up as a standard operating procedure.

  3. 3

    You approve it, then we start

    We send the SOP to you for sign-off. Work on your files begins only once you have approved it.

Step two: every file

Sent today, back within 24 hours

  1. 01

    Submit your order

    Send the file the way your team already works. No new portal to learn.

  2. 02

    We process it

    Inside your software and templates, following your SOP.

  3. 03

    Quality check

    Every order is reviewed before it comes back to you.

  4. 04

    Fast delivery

    Standard turnaround is 24 hours, with evening and weekend coverage.

Step three: your review

You re-check everything, then close

We type everything so your team only has to re-check it. You review our work before it goes out, then get back to closings and client relationships.

Confidential
