On November 12, 2026, Qualia introduced Qualia Closer. According to [Qualia's announcement](https://blog.qualia.com/introducing-closer/), it works a title and escrow file from the day the order opens to the day it closes, inside the firm's own Qualia account.

For anyone who runs title production, that is a big claim. This article sets out what Qualia actually announced, who can use it today, what Qualia says stays with people, and what a production team can do now.

## In short

- Closer is in an invitation-only pilot. It is not generally available.
- It prepares work for review. People at the firm still issue commitments and policies.
- Written procedures matter more, not less, when work is handed to any outside worker.

## What Qualia announced

Qualia describes Closer as a "drop-in worker". In its announcement, the company says Closer secures a title search, prepares a preliminary exam for the customer to review, helps clear requirements, chases payoffs, balances the closing disclosure with the lender and schedules the signing. It also answers the parties on a file by email or text.

Qualia says Closer runs on top of the products its customers already use, including Qualia Core for the order and title work and Qualia Clear for email.

The company reports that Closer has worked live purchase and refinance files at 42 title agencies and law firms across 23 states since October, and that the first file it worked closed on October 30. Those are Qualia's own figures.

## Who can use Qualia Closer today

Not most firms, yet. Qualia says the Closer Pilot Program is invitation-only and that Closer is not being made generally available at this stage. Firms will be added over the coming weeks, starting with existing Qualia customers, and there is a waitlist for those who want to be considered.

If your office runs on Qualia, that means watching for an invitation. If you run on SoftPro or RamQuest, nothing changes in your software today.

## What stays with your team

Qualia is direct about the limits. In the announcement, the company says a person at the firm records documents, disburses funds and issues final commitments and policies. Closer can prepare commitments and policies, but it does not issue them. Firm attorneys make the legal calls, and the firm's team oversees the work and approves what matters.

That is the part every title company should read twice. Whoever prepares the work, software or people, the firm that issues it is still the one that reviews it and stands behind it.

## What this means for title production

Three practical points follow from the announcement.

**Review becomes the core skill.** If more of the typing and chasing is prepared for you, the valuable work is checking it: does the vesting match the search, are the requirements complete, are the exceptions in your wording. That is true whether the preparation comes from Closer, from an in-house processor or from an outside production team.

**Your procedures have to be written down.** Qualia says Closer works the way each firm works. Any worker can only do that if the firm's way is recorded somewhere: how files are named, which exceptions are standard, what your underwriter requires. Many offices carry this in people's heads.

**Most files still need people this quarter.** The pilot is closed to most firms, and commitments and policies still have to go out every day in the meantime.

## What to do now

1. **Write your SOP.** Take one clean file of each type and record how your office handles it from order entry to final policy. This is useful on its own and it is the first thing any outside worker will need.
2. **Decide who re-checks prepared work.** Name the person who reviews commitments and policies before they are issued, and what they check.
3. **Keep production moving.** If typing is backing up now, do not wait for a pilot invitation to fix it. Extra production capacity and automation are not either-or choices.
4. **If you are on Qualia, join the waitlist** and ask your account contact what the pilot requires.
