On November 12, 2026, Qualia introduced Qualia Closer. According to [Qualia's announcement](https://blog.qualia.com/introducing-closer/){:target="_blank" rel="noopener"}, it works a title and escrow file from the day the order opens to the day it closes, inside the firm's own Qualia account.

## What Qualia announced {#announced}

Qualia says Closer secures a title search, prepares a preliminary exam for the customer to review, helps clear requirements, chases payoffs and schedules the signing. The company reports that it has worked live files at 42 firms across 23 states since October.

## Who can use it today {#who}

Not most firms, yet. The pilot is by invitation, starting with existing Qualia customers, and Qualia has opened a waitlist.

## What stays with your team {#stays}

Qualia is clear that a person at the firm records documents, disburses funds and issues final commitments and policies, and that attorneys make the legal calls. Review and approval remain human work.
