What We Offer

Comprehensive Title Solutions

From order entry to final policy, we handle every step with precision and care.

Title order entry

We open and build new files in your title production software, entering property, party and transaction details from the order documents and flagging anything missing before the file moves on.

Learn more about order entry

Final policies

After closing, we prepare the owner's and lender's final policies with recording information and satisfied requirements, so your team only has to re-check them and issue.

Learn more about final policies

Title examination and commitments

We examine the search package your team supplies and type the commitment: Schedule A, requirements and exceptions. You make the underwriting decisions and issue the commitment.

Learn more about examination and commitments

Title search preparation

We get files ready for your searcher or abstractor: legal description, parcel ID, names to search and prior policies, organized the way your team expects. We prepare searches; we do not run them.

Learn more about search preparation

About Us

Your Trusted Partner in Title Services

Jade Title Services was founded with a clear mission: to provide title companies with a reliable, affordable partner for their back-office title needs. We understand that every order matters, and we treat each one with the care and attention it deserves.

Our team, led by founder and Senior Title Examiner Ali Abbas Kamran, has worked in title support since 2019. We handle title order entry, title search preparation, title examination and commitments, and final policies, working inside your software and following your procedures.

Whether you're a small title office or a large operation, we scale to meet your needs, with flexible hours including evenings and weekends and rates tailored to your volume.
